The band you are aiming at For your profile, the realistic first landing is the 9 to 15 LPA band. That is the TCS internal drive floor and the junior external range. Do not plan your life around the 70 LPA number. Plan it around the 9 to 15 LPA floor and the 25 to 50 LPA second step.

Who is hiring, and how they hire

Three tiers. The big IT services firms are hiring into AI-specific pods. TCS runs a 2026 skill-based drive for 2 to 4 years of experience at 9 LPA and up, with a 50 thousand joining bonus. It is cutting bench and delivery roles while it grows AI teams. Infosys, Wipro, HCL, and Tech Mahindra are doing the same dance.

Then the product and startup tier. Sarvam AI, Krutrim, and Gnani. India's AI startups raised 1.07 billion dollars in the first half of 2026, up 33 percent from the year before. These firms hire aggressively and pay in equity.

Finally, the global-remote frontier labs. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Palantir hire applied engineers in India, paying in rupee-denominated bands that still beat most domestic product roles. Palantir's forward-deployed engineer role sits at a 211 thousand dollar median in the US. The India remote version is a fraction of that, but it is the strongest India-based remote channel in 2026.

How they screen has changed In 2026, hiring is portfolio-first. A GitHub with shipped projects, a Kaggle ranking, and a deployed system outrank a wall of certificates. A certificate tells them you watched a course. A repo tells you you built a thing. Build the thing.
